# MRI-Based Quantification of Hemispheric Asymmetry in Alzheimer’s Disease
This repository contains the code for the BMEG-E511 Biomedical Image Processing Final Project, titled 'MRI-Based Quantification of Hemispheric Asymmetry in Alzheimer’s Disease'. In this project, T1-weighted MRI scans from the ADNI database were used to investigate whether MRI-derived hemispheric asymmetry indices could serve as interpretable imaging biomarkers for classifying Alzheimer’s disease progression.

## Dataset
The dataset used in this study was obtained from the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) database, accessed via the Image and Data Archive (IDA) maintained by the Laboratory of Neuro Imaging (LONI) at the University of Southern California (https://adni.loni.usc.edu). ADNI is a large multicenter longitudinal research initiative designed to investigate the progression of Alzheimer’s disease using neuroimaging, clinical, genetic, and biomarker data.

The neuroimaging data used in this study consisted of whole-brain T1-weighted (T1W) Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) scans obtained from the ADNI database. A total of 209 MRI scans were downloaded through the ADNI search interface. Participants were grouped into three diagnostic categories for analysis: cognitively normal (CN), mild cognitive impairment (MCI), and Alzheimer’s disease (AD). The dataset included 31 unique subjects consisting of 14 females and 17 males, ranging in age from 60 to 93 years. For this project, the unit of analysis was defined as a subject-visit sample, representing one processed scan per subject per visit.
